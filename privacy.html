Website Privacy Policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how lostpinescreative.com (this website) handles visitor information, and how our business phone lines handle calls and text messages (§7). It is operated by Lost Pines Creative LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), a Texas limited liability company based in Bastrop, Texas.

Our mobile apps (NomadCore, Remember Who, Evident House, Sovereign School, and the Lost Pines app for Groundwork and DeSmit Designs customers) are covered by separate, app-specific privacy policies and by our Terms of Service. This website policy does not describe those apps.

2. Analytics we use

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-80VW0JE7HW) to understand general traffic to the site — which pages are visited and how visitors find us — so we can improve it. Analytics cookies and identifiers are set only after you accept the cookie notice (see §3).

When enabled, Google Analytics may process:

  • Pages viewed, time on page, and the site or link that referred you
  • Approximate, city-level location derived from your IP address (Google does not log or store your full IP address for Google Analytics, and we never see it)
  • General device and browser type, screen size, and language

We do not use analytics to collect your name, email, or any information that directly identifies you, and we do not attempt to re-identify visitors.

3. Cookies & your consent

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Analytics storage is denied by default. Until you choose — and if you Decline — no analytics cookies or identifiers are stored on your device, and Google Analytics receives only cookieless, aggregated signals (such as a page view and your consent status) that cannot identify you or build a persistent profile. Only if you click Accept are standard analytics cookies set and full measurement enabled.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage so the notice does not reappear. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and reload — the notice will show again.

4. How we use the information

Analytics information is used only in aggregate, to understand overall site traffic and improve content and performance. We do not:

  • serve advertising or build advertising profiles
  • request advertising or ad-personalization consent — those signals stay denied by default and this site never asks to enable them
  • sell your personal information, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising

5. Google as our analytics processor

Google Analytics processes the information above on Google's infrastructure as our service provider. Advertising and ad-personalization signals are denied by default under Consent Mode and are never requested by this site, so Google Analytics is used for traffic measurement only. Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn about Google Analytics data practices and opt out browser-wide via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process standard server request logs (including IP addresses) for security and to deliver the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Phone calls & text messages

When you call a Lost Pines Creative business line (including DeSmit Designs) and we can't pick up, the call may be answered by an AI receptionist, which tells you it is an AI and that the call may be recorded. To take your message, book a time, and follow up, we process your phone number, what you tell the assistant, a recording and transcript of the call, and an AI-generated summary. If you hang up or we miss your call, we may send one text message to the number you called from so you can reach us; texts you send back are delivered to us. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time, or HELP for help. So the business can call you back quickly, it is alerted to each call by text and email with your name, number, and a short summary of what you need; the recording and full transcript stay in its secure portal.

These services are provided for us by Twilio (phone and text delivery), Vapi (call handling, speech-to-text and voice), Anthropic (the AI model), Cal.com (appointment booking), Resend (alert email delivery), and Supabase (our database). They process this information only to provide the service to us. We do not sell your phone number or share it, or your text-messaging opt-in, with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Call recordings and full transcripts are deleted after 180 days; the short summary of your request is kept as part of our customer records.

8. Data retention

User- and event-level Google Analytics data is retained for no longer than 14 months, after which Google deletes it; aggregated statistics may persist in Google's reports. We do not maintain a separate database of website visitors. Call and text records are covered in §7.

9. Your choices & rights

You can decline analytics at any time (§3), clear this site's cookies/local storage, or use the browser opt-out add-on (§5). Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request or ask a question, contact us (§11).

10. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them through the site.
