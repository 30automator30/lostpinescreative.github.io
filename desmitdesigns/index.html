Services

From concept sketches to finished products

3D Printing

Multi-color and multi-material FDM printing (up to 16 colors), plus high-resolution 12K resin printing for ultra-fine detail. Functional prototypes, art pieces, custom gifts, signage, miniatures, and production parts.

CAD & Engineering Design

Professional 3D modeling and engineering drawings using SolidWorks and Blender. 15+ years of design engineering experience backing every project.

Laser Cutting & Engraving

Precision laser cutting and engraving on wood, acrylic, leather, and more. Custom signs, personalized gifts, and detailed artwork.

Product Development

End-to-end product development from napkin sketch to manufactured part. Prototyping, iteration, and design-for-manufacturing consulting.

Graphic Design & Branding

Logo design, business cards, product labels, and brand identity packages. Digital and print-ready deliverables.

Custom Commissions

Have an idea? Let's make it real. Custom one-off pieces, personalized gifts, themed decorations, and specialty items built to your specifications.

About

DeSmit Designs is a design and fabrication studio based in Bastrop, Texas, specializing in turning ideas into tangible products.

With over 15 years of professional engineering experience spanning medical devices, consumer products, and industrial design, Daniel DeSmit brings a depth of technical skill rarely found in small-shop fabrication.

Whether you need a one-off custom gift, a prototype for your product idea, or production-quality branded items, DeSmit Designs delivers with engineering precision and creative flair.
