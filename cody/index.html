Meet Cody

A gentle desk-pet friend for young children. He knows their name, plays games, tells stories and dances — all on your phone or tablet, with no accounts, no ads, and nothing he hears ever recorded.

What Cody does

  • Talks with a real recorded voice and says your child's name, their birthday and their pets' names.
  • Hears claps, music and his name. Say "Cody!" and ask him the time, a fact, or to dance; clap and he claps back.
  • Plays games at easy, medium or hard: peekaboo, feeding friends, sorting, Go Fish, tic-tac-toe, a marble run, letters, block math, telling the time, drumming and singing.
  • Tells stories — interactive adventures your child taps, says and claps through, Bible stories, and a calm bedtime story.
  • School time on weekdays: a few short subjects to pick from, with a gold star at the end.
  • Little everyday moments: a plant to water, friends who come to visit, a counting parade, dance parties, timers and a calendar page.

Made for families

  • No accounts, no ads, and no chat with strangers.
  • The microphone stays on the device. Sound is checked in tiny slices and dropped right away: never recorded, saved or sent.
  • Your family's details are encrypted on your device and never uploaded.
  • Works offline. Only the optional radio needs the internet.
  • A weekly play report for you, behind a grown-up question in Settings: what your child played and what they talked to Cody about.

Read exactly what stays on the device and what doesn't in the Cody privacy policy.

Help & support

  • Cody can't hear? Check that microphone access is on for Cody in your device's Settings. He still works without it, he just can't hear claps or his name.
  • Change your child's name or pets: tap the gear on Cody's home screen and answer the grown-up question.
  • Start over: deleting the app deletes everything Cody knows.
