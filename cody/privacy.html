Cody Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

Cody ("the app") is a desk-pet companion for young children, made by Lost Pines Creative LLC, a Texas limited liability company in Bastrop, Texas ("we", "us"). This policy covers the app on iPhone, iPad and Android. It is written for parents and guardians, who set the app up. Our website is covered by our website privacy policy.

In short: Cody has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you tell Cody about your family stays on your device. The only things that leave the device are the radio stream when your child asks for the radio (§5) and, if the app crashes, a technical crash report with nothing about your child in it (§4).

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Your family: your child's first name and birthday, and your pets' names and kinds, which you enter when setting Cody up so he can greet your child and say their names.
  • Play and memory: the games played and their levels, the garden, calendar events you add, and a short record of what Cody understood your child say (words, never sound) so he can remember and answer.
  • The weekly play report: how long and what your child played, and the words Cody understood, shown to you in Settings behind a grown-up question.

All of this is stored only on the device, encrypted with AES-256 under a key kept in the device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). It is never uploaded, synced or shared, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. The microphone

With your permission, Cody listens so he can hear claps, music, singing and his name. Sound is processed on the device in short slices of a fraction of a second and then dropped. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Word recognition runs on the device and only knows a fixed list of words Cody understands. Words count only right after your child says "Cody", taps the mic button, or is asked to say something on a story page; anything else is thrown away. The microphone is off whenever the app isn't on screen. You can turn microphone access off at any time in your device's Settings; Cody still works, he just can't hear.

When Cody says a name he doesn't have a recording for, the device's own built-in voice reads it out; the name isn't sent anywhere by the app.

4. Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix crashes. A crash report contains only technical information:

  • what went wrong and where in the app's code;
  • the app version, the operating system and its version, the device model, and technical state at the time of the crash (such as available memory);
  • a random identifier created by the crash-reporting tool for this installation, so we can tell whether the same crash keeps happening. It is not linked to your child, your family, or any advertising identifier.

A crash report does not include your child's name or birthday, your pets, anything Cody heard or said, the play record, the device's name, or any account or contact information (the app has none). We've turned off the optional parts of the crash tool that could reveal more: screenshots, screen layouts, a trail of taps or screens, performance tracing and usage sessions, and we don't store IP addresses with reports.

We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs in Cody. They are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Sentry processes them on our behalf under the Sentry Privacy Policy.

5. The radio

If your child asks Cody to play the radio, the app streams the Air 1 radio station over the internet from its broadcaster. Like any internet radio, the broadcaster's streaming service receives the device's IP address so it can send the audio; the app sends nothing else. That is the only station Cody can play, and the radio turns itself off after 30 minutes.

6. What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, in-app ads or ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of information about you or your child.
  • No chat with other people, and no way for your child to post or share anything.

7. Children

Cody is made for young children, to be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian. What your family enters stays encrypted on the device, and sound from the microphone never leaves it. The only thing the app sends us is a crash report (§4); its random installation identifier is used only to support the app's internal operation (finding and fixing crashes), as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) allows, and never to contact, profile or advertise to anyone.

8. Your choices

You can change or remove your family's details in Settings (behind the grown-up question), turn microphone access off in your device's Settings, and delete everything by deleting the app. If you'd like a crash report from your device deleted sooner, contact us (§10) and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above. If a change means more information would leave your device, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
