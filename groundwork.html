Questions

Do I need to be "techy" for this?

No. That's the whole point — you run your business, I run the tech. Everything is set up for you and explained in plain language.

Are you a marketing or ad agency?

No. I don't run ads or chase leads — I'm a systems-and-integration engineer. I build the system that handles your leads: the website, booking, payments, AI, and the wiring between them. If you need ads too, I'll happily point you to someone who does that.

Do I own my accounts and data?

Yes, always. Your website, payment account, domain, and data are yours. I set them up in your name and manage them; you're never locked in.

What's the free digital audit?

A short conversation and a written rundown of the tools you're running, where the gaps are, and exactly what I'd connect or fix first — no obligation, and it's yours to keep.

Is the AI going to sound like a robot to my customers?

No. The AI receptionist is set up to sound like your business, capture the details, and hand real leads to you. It catches the calls you'd otherwise miss, and I keep it tuned.

What if I don't want AI, or don't have it?

No problem — AI is optional. The heart of what I do is connecting your tools so they talk to each other, which doesn't need AI at all. I can build your whole system integration-only, and add the AI receptionist later if you ever decide you want it.

What does it really cost?

You can start small — a Starter setup is $750 — then a one-time build plus a monthly care plan as you grow. The free audit gives you a firm number for your specific situation before you commit to anything.
