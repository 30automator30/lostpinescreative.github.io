Questions

Do I need to be "techy" for this?

No. You run your business, I run the tech. Everything is set up for you and explained in plain language.

Are you a marketing or ad agency?

No. I don't run ads or chase leads. I'm a systems-and-integration engineer who builds the system that handles your leads. If you need ads too, I'll point you to someone who does that.

Do I own my accounts and data?

Yes, always. Your website, payment account, domain, and data are yours. I set them up in your name and manage them; you're never locked in.

What's the free digital audit?

A short conversation and a written rundown of your tools, the gaps, and what I'd connect or fix first. No obligation, and it's yours to keep.

Will the AI sound like a robot to my customers?

No. The AI receptionist is set up to sound like your business, capture the details, and hand real leads to you. I keep it tuned.

What if I don't want AI?

No problem, AI is optional. Connecting your tools doesn't need it. I can build your whole system integration-only and add the receptionist later if you want it.

What does it really cost?

A Starter setup is $750, then a one-time build plus a monthly care plan as you grow. The free audit gives you a firm number before you commit to anything.

What is the monthly charge for?

Hosting, updates, backups, security monitoring, support, small monthly changes, and a plain-language report. Higher tiers add the AI receptionist, automatic review requests, and priority support. There's no long-term lock-in, since you own every account.
