Lost Pines App Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

The Lost Pines app ("the app") is made by Lost Pines Creative LLC, a Texas limited liability company in Bastrop, Texas ("we", "us"). It lets customers of our Groundwork and DeSmit Designs services see their calls, messages, setup and projects. This policy covers the app on iPhone and Android. Our website and business phone lines are covered by our website privacy policy; use of our services is covered by our Terms of Service.

2. Information the app uses

  • Your account: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), and the name and phone number you add on the Account screen.
  • Your business records (Groundwork): the calls, voicemails, missed calls and leads your AI receptionist or phone records for your business — including the caller's name and number, a short summary and, for receptionist calls, a transcript and recording link — plus your reception settings, requests you send us, and the services we set up for you.
  • Your projects (DeSmit Designs): project details, quotes, status updates, notes, and files or photos you or we add.
  • Text-Back (Android version we install for you only): to text people who call you and don't reach you, the app reads your phone's call log and checks your contacts on your phone. Only missed-call events leave the phone: the caller's number, the time, and whether a text was sent. Your call log and contacts are never uploaded. Texts go out from your own number through your phone. Text-Back is not part of the iPhone app or the Play Store app.

The app does not use your location, does not access your camera, microphone or photo library on iPhone, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How we use it

Only to provide the service you signed up for: signing you in, showing your business's records and projects, sending you alerts about new calls (by email, and by text where your plan includes it), handling your requests, and keeping the service secure. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

4. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in and file storage (encrypted in transit; row-level security means each customer can only see their own records).
  • Resend — delivers sign-in, password and new-call alert emails.
  • For the AI receptionist and text-back on our phone lines: Twilio, Vapi, Anthropic and Cal.com, as described in §7 of our website privacy policy.

They process information only to provide the service to us. We share information otherwise only if the law requires it.

5. What stays on your phone

The app keeps your sign-in session and the names of your businesses on the phone so it can open offline; your inbox and projects are loaded fresh each time and not stored on the phone. On Android, the app's data is excluded from cloud backups. The Text-Back version stores its phone token and settings encrypted, and keeps phone numbers on the phone only in hashed or encrypted form.

6. How long we keep it

Your account information is kept until you delete your account. Business records (calls, messages, invoices and project history) are kept as part of the service while it's active. Call recordings and full transcripts are deleted after 180 days.

7. Deleting your account and your choices

You can delete your account in the app (Account ▸ Delete account) or on the web at lostpinescreative.com/delete-account.html. Deleting removes your login, your profile and your own DeSmit Designs projects and files. Records that belong to a business we serve (such as its call history) are detached from your login and kept for that business. You can turn off call-alert emails in Setup, switch the AI receptionist and Text-Back off, and ask us for a copy of, or a correction to, your information.

8. Children

The app is for business customers and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is significant.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: ddesmit@lostpinescreative.com · Lost Pines Creative LLC, Bastrop, Texas.
