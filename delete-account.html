Delete your account

This deletes the login you use for the Lost Pines app, the Groundwork client portal and the DeSmit Designs customer portal. It can't be undone.

The fastest way: in the app

Open the Lost Pines app → Account → Delete account, then type DELETE. It's finished right away.

What happens to your data

  • Your login, profile and service requests are deleted.
  • DeSmit Designs projects you own — with their updates, milestones and files — are deleted. You're removed from projects other people shared with you.
  • Groundwork business services (your phone line, call inbox, reports and setup) are not deleted with your login, because they belong to your business and may be under a care plan. Your login is detached from them and any phones running Text-Back stop texting. We'll contact you to close or hand over the service; to have those records deleted too, tell us at ddesmit@lostpinescreative.com.
  • Billing records we're required to keep for tax purposes are kept as the law requires.
